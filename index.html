Good to know

Will my hair be dried afterwards?

Yes. Every treatment finishes with a gentle 57°C ionic blow dry, so you leave with clean, dry hair.

Is there an extra charge for long hair?

An additional $20+ may apply for hair longer than shoulder length.

Which treatment should I choose?

First visit or short on time: the 60-minute Experience. For dry or treated hair, a sensitive scalp or a special occasion: the 90-minute Premium, with hair mask, face mask and scalp exfoliation.

How do I book?

Book online any time, or call 0494 748 660. We recommend booking ahead, especially for weekends and Thursday evenings.
