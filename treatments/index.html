Good to know

Questions

How much is a head spa at Gold Class Head Spa Chermside?

Head Spa Essential (30 minutes) is $119, Head Spa Experience (60 minutes) is $199 and Head Spa Premium (90 minutes) is $299. An additional $20 applies for hair longer than shoulder length.

Which ritual should I choose?

The 30-minute Essential is a first taste or a short break. The 60-minute Experience is our most popular and the best place to start. The 90-minute Premium adds more time for dry or treated hair, a sensitive scalp, or a special occasion.

Where is Gold Class Head Spa Chermside?

Shop 403, Level 1, Westfield Chermside, 395 Hamilton Road, Chermside QLD 4032, near Best&Less.

Where do I park?

The Lime car park at Westfield Chermside is the easiest option.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Wednesday and Friday 9am to 5:30pm, Thursday 9am to 9pm, Saturday 9am to 5pm and Sunday 10am to 4pm.

Is my hair washed and dried?

Yes. Your hair is washed and dried before you leave, so you can go straight back to your day.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment.

Can I buy a gift card?

Yes. eGift cards are delivered by email and are valid at Gold Class Head Spa Chermside for three years from the date of purchase.
