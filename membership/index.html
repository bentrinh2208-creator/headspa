Good to know

Questions

How do I join?

Join online with the sign-up link and choose Subscription at checkout, or ask our team after your ritual and we will set it up for you in store. Your first $29 payment is taken on the day you join.

What is the welcome bonus?

During your first month of membership, every visit includes one complimentary treatment of your choice: a nourishing face mask, an intensive hair mask or a scalp exfoliation. Let our team know which one at the start of your ritual. Head Spa Premium already includes all three, so during your first month your 90 minute Premium is $230 instead (+$20 for hair longer than shoulder length), then $249 from month 2.

When can I use member prices?

Member prices apply to your rituals once your membership is active. Book online or call as usual, then let our team know you are a VIP Member and we apply your member price at checkout.

Is there a minimum term?

Yes. The membership has a minimum of 3 months ($87 in total). After 3 months it continues month to month and you can cancel at any time.

How do I cancel after 3 months?

Call us on 0494 748 660 or speak to our team in store and we will take care of it.

Is there an extra charge for long hair?

Yes. A $20 surcharge applies for hair longer than shoulder length, the same as our standard prices.

Can I combine member prices with gift cards or other offers?

No. Member prices are not combined with gift vouchers, coupons or other offers.

Can I use my membership at other Gold Class locations?

No. The VIP Membership is valid at Gold Class Head Spa Chermside only.
